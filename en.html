1 2 3
Power-upsMagnet, apple turbo and safety vest.

The hunter is after you

The double-barrel shotgun waits at the bottom of the screen. Every stumble brings it closer. When your lane glows red, switch lanes fast.

Every 5,000 m the Head Hunter shows up. He fires at two lanes at once for 25 seconds. Survive and you earn up to 1,500 coins.

In between there are bonus stretches full of coins and night runs where only your lantern lights the way.

Your runners

36 animals from around the world, each with its own ability

From the fox of the Summer Forest to the polar bear of the Arctic. Lion, tiger, kangaroo and jaguar guard their worlds and join you once you reach them. Every run earns your animal stars. Five stars bring golden sparkle and more coins.

💎 Rarity

Five tiers. How often they drop from the skin crate:

Common 70%Rare 20%Epic 7%Legendary 2.5%Mythic 0.5%

✨ Shine

A value from 0.000 to 1.000. The higher it is, the more the skin shines. From 0.600 a streak of light sweeps across it.

MatteSilkyGlossyRadiantFlawless

🎲 Pattern #0 to #999

Sets the hue, spots and stripes, the sparkle effects and the neon color. #0 to #4 are called “Prototype”, #777 is the “Lucky Number”.

🏃 Tracker

10% of all skins come with a tracker. It counts every meter you run wearing that skin.

🔢 Serial number

No. 1, 2, 3 … in the order the skins were pulled. Low numbers are the oldest.

🎁 Pulled by

Every skin remembers forever who pulled it, even after 400 sales. It also shows how often it has changed owners.

🏪 Market and trading

Sell skins to other players or trade with friends. Payment is only in Forest Talers earned by playing.

⬆️ Trade-up

10 skins of the same rarity become 1 skin of the next tier. Its shine is the average of the 10.

📖 Skin book

The skin book shows which variants you are still missing. Show off your best skins on your profile.

    FAQ

    Good to know

    Does Waldlauf cost anything?

    No. Waldlauf is completely free. There are no real-money purchases. You earn all coins, Forest Talers, crates, outfits and skins by playing.

    Are there ads?

    No, not a single one. No banners, no videos, nothing interrupts your run.

    Do I need to install anything?

    No. Waldlauf runs right in your browser. If you like, add it to your home screen like an app.

    How do I get rare outfits and skins?

    From crates: outfits with coins, skins with Forest Talers. You earn both by running, from daily rewards and in events. You can also buy skins on the market or trade with friends.

    What do shine, pattern and tracker mean?

    See the skins section above, or the “❓ Info” tab in the in-game skin shop.

    Will I lose my progress?

    It stays on your device. Use the in-game cloud save to move it to a new phone.
