## English

**Full description:**
Run for your fur! 🦊

A hunter is right behind you. Jump over logs, slide under branches, dodge rocks and collect coins as you race through 16 colorful worlds: from the summer forest to the savanna, the jungle and the cloud land.

🎮 WHAT AWAITS YOU
